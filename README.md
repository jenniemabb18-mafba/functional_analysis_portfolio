## Strategic Deliverables & Case Studies

### 1. Regulatory Governance & Cryptographic Architectures

#### **Stateless Tokenization & CDE Partitioning Engine**
* **Artifacts:** [Tokenization Architecture Board](./PCI-DSS_Tokenization_Architect_Board.jpg) • [Compliance Translation Matrix](./PCI-DSS_GDPR_Compliance_Translation_Matrix.pdf) • [Matrix Scope](./PCI-DSS_GDPR_Compliance_Translation_Matrix_Scope.pdf)
* **Objective:** Design a decoupled database schema and stateless cryptographic engine to eliminate Sensitive Authentication Data (SAD) persistence and radically minimize the PCI DSS audit footprint.
* **Key Architecture:**
  * **Identity Vault (`Table 1`):** PII isolated and keyed via surrogate keys (`UID`).
  * **Account Mapping Layer (`Table 2`):** Decoupled translation bridge between profiles and card indexes (`CCid`).
  * **Prescriptive CDE (`Table 3`):** Hardened vault containing encrypted PANs acting as the primary audit boundary.
  * **Ephemeral Handshake:** Implemented cryptographic randomizers producing dynamic CVVs (dCVVs) with strict 90-second expirations in volatile memory.
* **Framework Alignment:** PCI DSS v4.0.1 (Strict SAD non-persistence post-auth); GDPR Article 32 (Pseudonymization & Data Minimization).

#### **Artificial Neural Networks & International Privacy Law**
* **Artifact:** [GDPR White Paper: Privacy by Design in Automated Systems](./White_Paper_ANN_GDPR_Mabb.pdf)
* **Objective:** Evaluate the systemic friction between continuous machine learning / personalization engines and statutory privacy mandates.
* **Key Deliverables:** Formal legal-technical analysis assessing **Right to Erasure** and **Data Portability** inside non-linear learning models, establishing verifiable controls for algorithmic audit readiness.
